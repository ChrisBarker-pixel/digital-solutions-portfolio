SPROUT WING CHUN

The Little Idea.

In the Wing Chun system, Siu Nim Tao is the first and most vital form. Translated as the "Small Idea," it serves as the Seed for the entire system.

The Sprout represents the immense power of softness—the ability to penetrate the hard aspects of both self-defense and life through persistent growth. This is a system designed not just for combat, but for longevity. It preserves the body, strengthens the joints, muscles, and tendons, and allows a practitioner to remain effective well into their later years.

By combining the hard and soft aspects of traditional Chinese martial arts, Sprout Wing Chun offers a path that is beneficial for all stages of life. To ensure the highest fidelity of transmission, training is typically conducted one-on-one for the best results.
