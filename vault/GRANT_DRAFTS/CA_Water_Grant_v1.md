# CALIFORNIA WATER CONSERVATION GRANT PROPOSAL
## ENTITY: STARWASH (ID: 42-2183073)

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
This closed-loop integration will allow STARWASH to recover and reuse up to 85% of process water, 
drastically lowering the facility's dependence on municipal aquifers. By utilizing 
high-pressure, low-flow nozzle technology, we reduce per-vehicle consumption from 
40 gallons to under 12 gallons.

ECONOMIC IMPACT:
As a minority-owned mobile detailing business, this grant will facilitate the 
procurement of drought-resilient infrastructure, allowing for year-round 
operation during Stage 3 water restrictions in California.
